[Verse 1]
The captain went ashore at dawn
And never came aboard,
But still the Mary Ellen sailed
While all the north wind roared.

[Verse 2]
No hand was laid upon the wheel,
No bosun gave a cry,
Yet true she steered for southern seas
Beneath an empty sky.

[Verse 3]
And sailors say on moonlit nights
You'll see her running free,
Still searching for the man she lost
Across the rolling sea.
