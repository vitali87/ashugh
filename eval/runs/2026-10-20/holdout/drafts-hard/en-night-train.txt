[Verse 1]
Iron wheels on silver rails,
Mile by mile the moonlight pales,
Wheat lies sleeping, black and wide,
Not a single light outside.

[Chorus]
Oh, the night train is rolling along
Through the dark where the prairie lies still,
And the rhythm of wheels is a song
That keeps time like a heart in the chill.
