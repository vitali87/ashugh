[Verse 1]
Dust on the floorboards and light through the shutters,
Notices posted: the last dance is here.
Somewhere a ribbon of paper still flutters,
Left from the ball at the end of the year.
Once there were hundreds of dancers all turning,
Once there were fiddles that played to the dawn,
Lanterns were glowing and candles were burning,
Now all the curtains are faded and drawn.

[Verse 2]
Just for tonight the old band will be playing,
One more slow waltz for the ones who remain;
Silver-haired lovers are gracefully swaying,
Here where they met, they are dancing again.
Soon all the plaster and gold will be falling,
Mirrors and ribbons and lanterns and all,
Still in the silence the music keeps calling:
One, two and three, round the empty old hall.
