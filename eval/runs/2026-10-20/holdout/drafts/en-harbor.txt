[Verse 1]
The harbor lights are dim and low,
The pier is gray with salt and rust,
The tides still come, the tides still go,
But no one waits here, only dust.

[Verse 2]
My father mended nets by hand
And sang the songs his father knew,
The boats came home to Kestrel Strand
With silver herring, bright and blue.

[Chorus]
Oh, where have all the sailors gone?
The bell is silent on the quay,
We keep the lantern burning on
For ships that never come from sea.

[Verse 3]
The salting sheds have closed their doors,
The children left for city lights,
The gulls still circle round the shores
And cry along the empty nights.

[Verse 4]
I walk along the wall at dawn
And count the moorings, one by one,
The nets lie folded on the lawn,
The rope is rotting in the sun.

[Chorus]
Oh, where have all the sailors gone?
The bell is silent on the quay,
We keep the lantern burning on
For ships that never come from sea.

[Bridge]
Perhaps one morning, through the gray,
A sail will rise beyond the bar
And bring the old ones home to stay,
Or so I tell the evening star.
