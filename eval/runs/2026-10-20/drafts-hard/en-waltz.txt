[Verse 1]
Dust on the mirrors and dust on the curtain,
Velvet gone gray at the edge of the stage,
Nobody speaks, but the dancers are certain,
Someone has written the end on the page.
Once there were hundreds of couples in satin,
Roses and ribbons and pearls in their hair,
Swaying all night to a band from Manhattan,
Waltzing till morning with never a care.

[Verse 2]
Now there's a padlock, the window is broken,
Pigeons are nesting where trumpeters played,
Only a ribbon is left as a token,
Tied to the rail where the last couple stayed.
Hold me once more as the music grows slower,
One, two and three, on the boards we know well,
Dip me down low, then a little bit lower,
Kiss me goodbye at the closing-time bell.
