[Verse 1]
We sailed from Bristol in the fog,
The captain's chair was bare,
His coat still hanging by the wheel,
His pipe still burning there.
The bosun swore he'd gone ashore,
The cook said he was drowned,
But still she ran before the wind
As if her way were found.

[Verse 2]
She swung her bow into the north
Through ice and silver rain,
The compass spun, the lantern swung,
The wheel turned round again.
We heard a whistle on the deck,
A tune we all knew well,
And not a man among the crew
Would dare to ring the bell.

[Verse 3]
At dawn we sighted Bristol light
And came in safe to shore,
His widow waiting on the quay
As she had stood before.
She smiled and said, "He's never left,
He brought you through the foam,
For he was lost a year ago,
And still he steers you home."
