[Verse 1]
Steady wheels on silver rails,
Northbound through the prairie gales,
Lonely windows black as coal,
Whistle crying through my soul.
Grain and fences, mile on mile,
Sleeping towns in single file,
Somewhere past the county line
Moonlight spills like summer wine.

[Chorus]
Oh the night train is calling me home,
Through the dark where the wild grasses sway,
Under stars, over fields where I roam,
Till I wake to the gold of the day.

[Verse 2]
Coffee cooling, lights turned low,
Frozen stubble, fields of snow,
Old man snoring by the door,
Soldier dreaming of the shore.
Crossing bells go ringing by,
Silos tall against the sky,
Hold me, rhythm, hold me tight,
Carry me across the night.
