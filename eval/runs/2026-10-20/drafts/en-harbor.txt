[Verse 1]
The gulls still circle round the pier,
The nets hang drying in the sun,
But not one sail has come this year,
The harbor bell rings out for none.
[Chorus]
So sing, my love, of ships gone by,
Of masts that rose like winter trees;
The lighthouse burns against the sky
And guides no keel across the seas.
[Verse 2]
The chandler's shop has shut its door,
The young ones leave on morning trains,
While old men sit along the shore
And count the rust on anchor chains.
