[Verse 1]
The gulls still wheel above the pier,
The ropes hang slack on rusted rings,
But no ship's horn has sounded here,
And no one on the jetty sings.

[Verse 2]
My father mended nets by hand,
My mother salted cod by night,
Now weeds grow up through drifted sand,
The lighthouse long has lost its light.

[Chorus]
Come sailing home, you long-lost ships,
The tide still turns, the harbor waits,
Your names are on the widow's lips,
And rust has eaten through the gates.

[Verse 3]
The young ones took the morning train
To cities where the money flows,
And we who stayed behind remain
To mend the nets that no one throws.

[Chorus]
Come sailing home, you long-lost ships,
The tide still turns, the harbor waits,
Your names are on the widow's lips,
And rust has eaten through the gates.

[Bridge]
I keep my lantern on the sill
In case a sail should cross the bay,
The sea is wide, the night is still,
And morning seems so far away.

[Chorus]
Come sailing home, you long-lost ships,

[Outro]
The gulls still wheel above the pier,
The ropes hang slack on rusted rings,
And I will keep on waiting here
To see what news the morning brings.
