[Verse 1]
Once in this ballroom the lovers were turning,
Round as the moon while the music would flow,
Lanterns were glowing and hearts were all burning,
Nobody thought they would ever let go.

[Verse 2]
Now there are boards where the windows were gleaming,
Now there's a chain and a lock on the door,
Still, if you listen, the fiddles are dreaming,
Playing one waltz for the ghosts on the floor.
