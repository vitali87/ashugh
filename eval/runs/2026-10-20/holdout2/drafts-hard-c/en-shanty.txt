[Verse 1]
The captain went ashore at dawn
And never came aboard,
But still the Mary slipped her chain
And sailed without her lord.

[Verse 2]
No hand was on the wheel that night,
No boy to ring the bell,
Yet true she held her northern course
Through fog and rolling swell.

[Verse 3]
So raise a glass to Captain Jack,
Wherever he may be;
The Mary doesn't need him now,
She's married to the sea.
