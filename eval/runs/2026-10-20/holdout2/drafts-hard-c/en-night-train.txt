[Verse 1]
Iron wheels across the plain,
Silver rails in silver rain,
Midnight lanterns swaying low,
Sleeping towns we'll never know.

[Chorus]
Oh, the whistle is calling the night,
And the prairie is silver and gray,
There's a lamp in a window, a light
In a house at the end of the way.

[Verse 2]
Coffee cooling in my hand,
Endless miles of empty land,
Mother's letter, folded tight,
Says the porch lamp burns all night.
