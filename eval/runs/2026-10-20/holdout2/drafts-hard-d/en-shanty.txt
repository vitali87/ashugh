[Verse 1]
We sailed at dawn from Bristol quay,
The tide was running high,
But no one stood upon the deck
To give the captain's cry.

[Verse 2]
His coat still hangs beside the wheel,
His boots are by the door,
Yet still she turns against the wind
The way she did before.

[Verse 3]
So haul away, my bonny lads,
And never ask her where,
A hand is on the helm tonight
But no one's standing there.
