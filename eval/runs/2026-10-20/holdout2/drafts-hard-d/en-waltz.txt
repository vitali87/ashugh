[Verse 1]
Once in the ballroom the music was flowing,
Lanterns were hung from the rafters above,
Couples went circling, their faces all glowing,
Spinning like leaves in the arms of their love.

[Verse 2]
Now there's a sign on the door that says closing,
Dust on the floor where the dancers once spun,
Chairs on the tables, the bandleader dozing,
One final waltz, and the evening is done.
