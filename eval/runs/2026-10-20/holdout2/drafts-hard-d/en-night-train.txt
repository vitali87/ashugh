[Verse 1]
Midnight engine through the dark,
Prairie wind and one red spark,
Wheat fields roll like golden seas,
Lantern swaying in the breeze.

[Chorus]
We are rolling away through the night,
Through the miles and the miles of the plain,
With the stars and the moon for our light,
And the rails sing their silver refrain.

[Verse 2]
Sleeping towns go drifting by,
Water towers touch the sky,
Somewhere far a mother waits,
Standing by the farmhouse gates.
