[Verse 1]
The gulls still circle round the bay,
The pier is gray with salt and rust,
The ropes are coiled and stowed away,
The anchors sleeping in the dust.
My father sailed at morning light
And came home singing with the tide,
But no one sails from here tonight,
The water's empty, cold and wide.

[Chorus]
The harbor waits, the harbor sleeps,
No masts are rising from the sea,
The lighthouse lonely vigil keeps
For ships that never come to me.

[Verse 2]
The chapel bell rings out at noon,
The widows wander by the pier,
They hum a half-forgotten tune
To boats that will not anchor here.
The shops are shuttered one by one,
The children leave for city lights,
The nets are drying in the sun
And no one mends them through the nights.

[Chorus]
The harbor waits, the harbor sleeps,
No masts are rising from the sea,
The lighthouse lonely vigil keeps
For ships that never come to me.

[Bridge]
But I will stay and keep the flame
And sing the songs my father knew,
And call each vessel by its name
Until the sea gives back the crew.
