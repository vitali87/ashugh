[Verse 1]
The Mary Jane put out to sea
Upon a morning tide,
Her captain's cabin empty stood,
No master at her side.
The bosun swore he heard him sing
The night before she sailed,
But when we woke, his bunk was cold,
And all our faces paled.

[Verse 2]
We hauled the lines and set the sails
With no one at the wheel,
And yet she held a steady course
As true as tempered steel.
The compass needle pointed north
Through fog and freezing spray,
And not a man among us knew
Who steered her on her way.

[Verse 3]
At last we raised a rocky isle
Where gulls cried overhead,
And on the shore a lantern swung
Above his ocean bed.
So drink to him who sails us home,
Though no one sees his face,
The Mary Jane will always find
Her captain's resting place.
