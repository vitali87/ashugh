[Verse 1]
The harbor lights still burn at night
Above a sea of empty blue,
The gulls still wheel in morning light,
But not a sail has come in view.

[Verse 2]
My father mended nets by hand
And sang the songs his father knew,
Now rope lies rotting on the sand
And salt has eaten through and through.

[Chorus]
Oh, ring the bell for ships at sea
That sailed away and never came,
The harbor waits for you and me
And calls the sailors all by name.

[Verse 3]
The chapel bell is cracked and still,
The tavern sign hangs in the rain,
The young have gone beyond the hill
And no one walks the pier again.

[Chorus]
Oh, ring the bell for ships at sea
That sailed away and never came,
The harbor waits for you and me
And calls the sailors all by name.
