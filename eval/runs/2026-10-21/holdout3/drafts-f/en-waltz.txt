[Verse 1]
Dust on the mirrors and curtains are falling,
Velvet gone grey and a scratch on the floor,
Once there was music and laughter and calling,
Now there's a padlock and sign on the door.
Here in the corner the orchestra waited,
There by the window we learned how to sway,
Shy as the morning and wildly elated,
Young as the music, we danced till the day.

[Verse 2]
Waltz with me once in the light that is fading,
Turn with me slowly the way that we did,
Hear the old band in the dark serenading,
Once more around, then they'll lower the lid.
Morning will come with the cranes and the hammers,
Taking the windows and boards one by one,
Still in my heart all the melody clamors,
Dancing goes on when the dancing is done.
