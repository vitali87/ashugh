[Verse 1]
The captain went below one night
And left us to the sea,
We found his coat upon the chair,
His bible and his key.
Yet still the sails went up at dawn
With no one at the wheel,
And still she cut the grey-green waves
Upon an even keel.

[Verse 2]
So haul away, my bonny boys,
She knows the way alone,
She steers by stars we cannot see
To waters none have known.
The bosun swears he heard him laugh
Within the creaking hold,
The cook has seen his lantern burn
When all the night is cold.

[Verse 3]
And if you meet her on the main
With canvas white as bone,
Then raise your cap and hold your tongue
And leave her to her own.
For somewhere past the edge of maps
Her captain waits ashore,
And all the sailors lost at sea
Will sing for evermore.
