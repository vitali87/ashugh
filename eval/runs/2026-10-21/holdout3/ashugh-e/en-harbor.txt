[Verse 1]
The gulls still circle over stone,
The lantern rusts above the quay,
The fishers mend their nets alone
And watch the empty, silent sea.

[Verse 2]
My father sailed at break of day
With salt and silver in his hand,
But now the timbers rot away
And weeds grow thick along the sand.

[Chorus]
Oh, no more ships will come to town,
No sails will crown the morning tide,
The harbor bell has rusted down
And all our songs have slowly died.

[Verse 3]
The tavern door is boarded tight,
The children left for distant streets,
We sit beneath the ghostly light
And hum the tune the wind repeats.

[Chorus]
Oh, no more ships will come to town,
No sails will crown the morning tide,
The harbor bell has rusted down
And all our songs have slowly died.

[Outro]
So light the lamp and hold it high,
In case a sail should cross the bay;
The sea forgets, but you and I
Will keep the harbor night and day.
