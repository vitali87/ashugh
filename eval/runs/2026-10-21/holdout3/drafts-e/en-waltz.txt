[Verse 1]
Dust on the floorboards and lamps that are fading,
Chairs on the tables and nobody here,
Once there were lovers in velvet parading,
Turning and turning for year after year.
Here was the orchestra, brass and piano,
Here was the corner where first we had met,
Here was the singer who sang in soprano
Songs that the city has learned to forget.

[Verse 2]
Monday the builders will come with their hammers,
Pulling the curtains and boarding the door,
Taking the mirrors, the lamps and the banners,
Nobody waltzes here anymore.
Give me your hand for the last of the dances,
One, two and three, as the music grows slow,
Lost in the glimmer of fading romances,
Hold me and never, oh never let go.
