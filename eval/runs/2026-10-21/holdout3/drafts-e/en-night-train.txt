[Verse 1]
Wheels are singing on the rail,
Prairie silver, cold and pale,
All the windows hold a star,
No one knows us where we are.

[Chorus]
Oh, the night train is rolling along
Through the dust and the dark of the plain,
And the engine is humming a song
Like a lullaby sung by the rain.

[Verse 2]
Towns go by without a name,
Lonely stations look the same,
Somewhere far a lantern glows,
Where it's going, no one knows.
